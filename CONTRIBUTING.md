# Contributing

Thank you for looking. Widsith Uploader's source is published so that anyone can read what the app does with their files, and that is what it is here for.

For now, pull requests are not merged. The app is young and changes quickly, and its author would rather keep one hand on it until it has settled. That may change later.

Questions, ideas and bug reports are welcome on [Discord](https://discord.gg/532DstKnNE) or by email to info@widsith.app.
